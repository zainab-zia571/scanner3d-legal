Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 September 2026

Introduction

This policy explains what the 3D Scanner Android application (com.scanner3d.app) does with information, and it is short for a simple reason: the application does not send anything anywhere.

Your scans stay on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising, and nothing you scan is uploaded anywhere. A scan leaves the application only as a file you deliberately share.

An earlier version of this policy said the application declared no INTERNET permission. That has changed, and it is stated here rather than quietly dropped. The application now declares it because Google Play's billing library requires it, and Google Play requires that library before a subscription can exist. The application itself opens no network connection — it collects nothing, sends nothing and has nothing to send to. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the version that changes it is released.

By using the application you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

Information We Collect

We collect nothing. The developer receives no data from the application — no personal information, no usage statistics, no crash reports, no device identifiers and no contact details. Nothing is transmitted, so there is nothing for us to receive or store.

The application does create and hold data on your device, described below. That data is yours; it is not sent to us.

Scan Data

Scanning is the purpose of the application, and it produces the most sensitive data it handles. A scan can include:

  • Depth measurements and three-dimensional point data.
  • Photographs taken during capture, used to colour the finished model. These are ordinary camera images and may show whatever was in view, including people, documents or the inside of your home.
  • Camera positions and orientations relative to where the scan began.
  • Reconstructed models, textures and any measurements you take.

All of it is written to the application's private storage on the device. Treat a scan as you would a photograph of the same place.

No location data. The application does not request location permission and does not record where a scan was taken. Positions inside a scan are relative to its own starting point and say nothing about where on Earth you were.

Device Information

The application checks what your device is capable of so that it can offer the scanning modes that will actually work: whether ARCore is available, whether a depth sensor is present, how much memory and free storage there is, and the camera's characteristics. This check happens on the device, is used only to decide what to show you, and is not transmitted.

Permissions

These are the permissions the installed application declares, in full.

  • Camera — the only permission you are asked to grant. It is required to see what you are scanning. Android will prompt for it, and you may withdraw it at any time in system settings, after which scanning will not function.
  • Internet (INTERNET) — required by Google Play's billing library, which the application includes so that a subscription can be offered in a future version. No code in this application opens a connection, and nothing you scan is transmitted.
  • In-app billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — permits the application to ask Google Play about purchases. There is nothing to purchase in this version.
  • View network connections (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — declared by Google's ARCore component, which the application includes, and not by the application itself. It permits checking whether a network exists. It does not permit using one, and no code in this application reads it.
  • An internal receiver permission (DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION) — generated automatically by the Android support libraries so that the application's own components cannot be reached by other applications. It grants no access to anything on your device.

These are not declared, and so cannot be used:

  • Location, contacts, microphone, phone, or external storage. Scans are kept in the application's own private directory. A file reaches your gallery or file manager only when you export or share it yourself.

Data Storage

Scans, photographs, models and measurements are stored in the application's private folder on your device, which the Android system keeps separate from other applications. They remain there until you delete them.

Deleting a scan in the application removes its files and its database entry from the device. Uninstalling the application removes everything it created. Because none of it was ever copied elsewhere, that is the end of it — there is no remote copy for us to delete on your behalf, and no request for us to action.

Export and Sharing

The application can write a model to a file in a number of standard formats, and hand that file to another application you choose — a messaging app, a cloud drive, an email client. This is the only way data leaves the application, and it happens only when you ask for it.

Once you have shared a file, it is governed by whatever terms apply to the application or service you shared it with, not by this policy. Consider what a scan shows before sending it.

The application provides no cloud backup and no synchronisation. If you want a copy of a scan kept elsewhere, exporting it is how you make one.

Third Party Services

The application uses Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), a Google component installed on your device, for motion tracking and depth estimation. ARCore runs on the device as part of the Android system. Its behaviour is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy rather than by this one:

  • policies.google.com/privacy
  • developers.google.com/ar/data-privacy

Beyond ARCore, the application integrates no third-party service: no analytics, no advertising network, no crash reporting, no social sign-in.

Security

Data is held in application-private storage, which Android protects from other applications. The strongest protection here is structural rather than technical: data that is never transmitted cannot be intercepted in transit, and data held on no server cannot be exposed by a breach of one. That remains true of this version — the application transmits nothing, whatever permissions the libraries it includes may declare.

What remains is the security of the device itself. A screen lock and full device encryption — both standard on modern Android — are what protect your scans if the phone is lost. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

User Rights

Rights of access, correction, deletion and portability normally require a request to whoever holds your data. Here you hold it, so you exercise them directly:

  • Access — open any scan in the application.
  • Portability — export a scan in a standard format.
  • Deletion — delete a scan, or uninstall the application.
  • Withdrawing consent — revoke the camera permission in Android settings.

If you would still like to make a request or ask a question about this policy, please contact us.

Children's Privacy

The application is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone — child or adult — because we collect nothing at all. Parents should be aware that scanning captures camera images of whatever is in view, and that those images stay on the device where they can be viewed and shared. We would encourage supervision on that basis rather than because of any data we hold.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. If a future version of the application ever collects or transmits data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described plainly rather than buried.
