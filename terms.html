Measurement Disclaimer

Measurements produced by this application are estimates, not survey data. Do not rely on them where an error would be costly, dangerous, or legally significant.

Dimensions, areas and distances are derived from camera imagery and motion tracking. Their accuracy varies with lighting, surface texture, the distance scanned, how thoroughly you covered the subject, and the capabilities of your particular device. Reflective, transparent and featureless surfaces are measured poorly or not at all.

Models produced by photogrammetry — that is, from photographs rather than depth — have no inherent real-world scale until you calibrate them against a known dimension. The application says so where this applies, and you should not treat such figures as metres until you have.

Do not use this application as the sole basis for construction, fabrication, manufacturing, insurance claims, legal proceedings, medical decisions, or any purpose where an inaccurate measurement carries real consequences. Verify anything that matters with an appropriate instrument.

Cloud Processing

Scanning and reconstruction run on your device, and that remains true whatever you choose here. Cloud processing is an optional extra: you may instead have a scan reconstructed on a remote machine, which is faster and can produce a better result than a phone can. It is off until you turn it on, and the application asks you to agree before the first upload. What is sent, where it goes and how long it is kept are set out in the privacy policy.

It depends on hardware and services we rent from others, and on your network. We do not promise that it will be available, that a job will succeed, or that it will finish in any particular time. A job may fail or be refused — because a scan carries too little to reconstruct, because capacity is unavailable, or because a limit has been reached. When one fails you are told, the charge for it is not consumed, and the scan on your phone is untouched and can still be reconstructed on the device.

Uploads and results are deleted within seven days. Cloud processing is not storage, not a backup, and not somewhere to keep anything.
