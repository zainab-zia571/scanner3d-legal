Measurement Disclaimer

Measurements produced by this application are estimates, not survey data. Do not rely on them where an error would be costly, dangerous, or legally significant.

Dimensions, areas and distances are derived from camera imagery and motion tracking. Their accuracy varies with lighting, surface texture, the distance scanned, how thoroughly you covered the subject, and the capabilities of your particular device. Reflective, transparent and featureless surfaces are measured poorly or not at all.

Models produced by photogrammetry — that is, from photographs rather than depth — have no inherent real-world scale until you calibrate them against a known dimension. The application says so where this applies, and you should not treat such figures as metres until you have.

Do not use this application as the sole basis for construction, fabrication, manufacturing, insurance claims, legal proceedings, medical decisions, or any purpose where an inaccurate measurement carries real consequences. Verify anything that matters with an appropriate instrument.
