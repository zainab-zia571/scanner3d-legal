3D Scanner

An Android application that captures three-dimensional scans of objects and rooms using the device camera, and builds models from them on the phone itself.

Everything stays on your device. The application holds no internet permission. It has no account, no server, and nothing to sign in to. Scans leave your phone only when you choose to share a file yourself.

What the application does

3D Scanner uses the rear camera, together with Google's ARCore motion tracking, to measure the shape of what the camera is pointed at. Those measurements are combined into a three-dimensional model, which you can view, measure, edit and export in a range of standard file formats.

All capture and all reconstruction happen on the device. No part of a scan is uploaded for processing, because the application has no means of uploading anything.

What it needs from your device

  • Camera — the only permission the application requests. It is required to see what you are scanning.
  • Storage on the device — scans are written to the application's own private folder, which other applications cannot read.
  • ARCore — Google Play Services for AR, where the device supports it, for motion tracking and depth.

Accuracy

Measurements taken from a scan are estimates produced from camera imagery. They are useful for planning and reference. They are not survey-grade and should not be relied upon where an error would be costly or unsafe. See the terms for the full statement.
