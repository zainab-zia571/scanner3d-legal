What to write about

  • Questions about the privacy policy or the terms.
  • Bugs, crashes, or a scan that came out wrong.
  • Features you would find useful.
  • Anything about how the application handles your data.

Reporting a problem

A report is far easier to act on with a few specifics. If you can, include:

  • Your device model and Android version.
  • The version of the application, shown in Settings under About.
  • Which scanning mode you were using.
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

Please do not attach a scan of anything private. A description of the problem is usually enough, and a scan may show more of your home or your documents than you intend.

Response times

This is a small independent project rather than a company with a support desk. Replies are usually sent within a few days, but there is no guaranteed response time, and messages may occasionally take longer.

Data requests

Requests to access or delete your data are usually best handled by you, on your device, because that is normally the only place your data exists. There is no account for us to look up. Deleting a scan, or uninstalling the application, removes it.

The exception is a scan you sent for cloud processing. A copy of that exists off your device until it is deleted, which happens automatically within seven days. If you would like one removed sooner, write to us and say which scan — the application can show you its job reference. See the privacy policy for detail.
