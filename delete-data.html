Deleting your data

Last updated 6 October 2026

There is no account to close. 3D Scanner has no sign-in, no profile and no user record, so there is nothing held under your name to delete. What there can be is a scan on your phone, and — only if you turned cloud processing on — a copy of one scan being worked on elsewhere. This page says how each of those goes away.

Scans on your phone

These are the ones that matter most, because they are the ones that exist by default. They are stored in the application's own private folder, which no other application can read.

  • Delete one scan. Open My Scans, press and hold the scan, and delete it. It moves to Recently Deleted and is removed for good after thirty days, or immediately if you choose Delete for good.
  • Delete everything. Uninstall the application. Android removes its private folder with it. Nothing survives the uninstall and nothing is restored if you install it again.

Anything uploaded for cloud processing

Cloud processing is off unless you switch it on and agree to it. If you never did, nothing has ever been uploaded and there is nothing here to delete.

If you did, the scan you chose to send is deleted automatically within seven days, along with the result. That is enforced by a deletion rule on the storage itself rather than by anybody remembering to run something, and it applies whether or not you ask.

  • Stop any further upload. In Settings, choose On this device. This takes effect immediately and also deletes the anonymous install identifier described in the privacy policy.
  • Have an upload deleted sooner than seven days. Write to the address below. Say roughly when you sent it and which scan it was, so the right one can be found.

Your subscription

If you subscribed, a record of that purchase is held by Google Play and by RevenueCat, who confirm for us whether a subscription is active. We do not hold your name, your email address or your card details, and we never see your card at any point.

Cancelling a subscription is done in the Google Play Store, under Subscriptions. Deleting the purchase record itself is a matter for Google, because it is their record of a payment you made to them, and they are required to keep some of it for tax and accounting reasons.

This is a small independent project rather than a company with a support desk. Replies are usually sent within a few days, but there is no guaranteed response time. Note that in most cases the seven-day rule will have removed an upload before a request could be acted on.

What is never collected, and so never needs deleting

No analytics, no crash reports, no advertising identifier, no device serial, no contacts, no location and no account. The full list is in the privacy policy; the short version is that the developer receives nothing about you at all, and the only thing that ever leaves your phone is a scan you chose to send.
